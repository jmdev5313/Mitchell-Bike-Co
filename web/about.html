Why a bike shop?

As a solutions consultant, I help large organizations understand how their service teams work, and build hands-on demos so they can try a new approach. I built Mitchell Bike Co. to bring that work into cycling, a world I love.

A bike shop has everything a real service operation has: cases, parts, suppliers, promises to customers, and a person who has to say yes. It's also small enough to understand in a minute. Every idea here comes from enterprise service work; only the setting changed.

What the Agent can't do

The shop's own code, not the model, decides what's allowed. The Agent can draft a message but never send it; only the Mechanic sends. It can quote, but only the Customer approves, from their own link. Nothing is ordered before that approval, and nothing is quoted that doesn't fit the bike in front of it. Every story on the demo stage is also an automated test of exactly these things.
